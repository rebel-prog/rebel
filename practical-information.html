Venue

The workshop takes place in Brno, Czech Republic, at Masaryk University, Faculty of Arts. The full room and address will be confirmed here before the event.

Getting to Brno

By train

Brno is well connected by rail. Direct trains run from Prague (about 2.5 hours), Vienna (about 1.5 hours), and Bratislava. The main station (Brno hlavní nádraží) is in the city centre.

By air

The nearest large airports are Vienna (VIE) and Prague (PRG), both with onward train or bus connections to Brno. Brno also has a small airport (BRQ) with limited routes.

Accessibility statement

Accessibility is central to this workshop's topic, and we take it seriously in practice. We aim to hold the event in a step-free, wheelchair-accessible venue with accessible toilets. On request we can arrange sign-language interpretation and live captioning, and we will provide a quiet space away from the main room.

If you have an access requirement — of any kind — please tell us through the registration form or the contact page. We would rather you ask than assume it can't be done.

This website is built to be accessible too: it works with a keyboard, works with screen readers, uses high-contrast text, and respects your system's reduced-motion setting. If something here gets in your way, please let us know.
