Registration will open in 2027

We expect registration to open after notifications of acceptance are sent, in summer 2027. Please check back, or write to us via the contact page to be notified.

Access and support

We want the workshop to be usable by everyone who wants to take part. The registration form will ask about access requirements — for example, sign-language interpretation, live captioning, dietary needs, or a quiet space. If you have a need not covered there, please contact us and we will do our best to arrange it.
